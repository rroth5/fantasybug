How do I find espn_s2 and SWID?
  1. On a computer, open fantasy.espn.com in Chrome and sign in.
  2. Press F12 (Windows) or Cmd + Option + I (Mac).
  3. Click Application at the top (behind » if you can't see it), then Cookies, then https://fantasy.espn.com.
  4. Type espn in the filter box. Copy the value next to espn_s2.
  5. Type SWID in the filter box. Copy its value, curly brackets and all.

Send them to your phone the way you like (a note to yourself works), or open this page on the computer instead: the TV's address is under the QR code.

These two codes act like your ESPN password. They stay on your TV and only ever go to ESPN. Don't post them anywhere.
