# Ideas for later

Not promises, just what would make fantasybug better, with what each would take.

- **A real phone app** (Android first, then iPhone). The phone remote is a web page served by the TV, so the TV
  whose icon you tap has to be on, and a page can't keep working offline. An app would find whichever TVs are on,
  never depend on one being awake, and could send touchdown alerts to the phone. Costs: store fees (Google $25 once,
  Apple $99 a year), store review (an app built on ESPN's data may be refused), and the tip button would have to
  go through the stores' payments.
- **Google Play for the TV app.** Easier to find and install, automatic updates. Same review and ESPN questions;
  first check whether a Play install gets "display over other apps" on Google TV.
- **Fire TV without a computer.** Fire TV has no "display over other apps" setting and doesn't list downloaded
  apps' accessibility services (tested on Fire OS 7.7). The Amazon Appstore might grant it; untested.
